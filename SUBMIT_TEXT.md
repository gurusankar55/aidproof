## Title
AidProof

## Description (≈530 chars)
Crypto relief drives raise money fast, but donors rarely see where it went and fake campaigns disappear. AidProof releases donations in tranches. Tranche 1 goes out after funding. Each next tranche needs validators to find a concrete spending report (items, amounts, receipts, beneficiaries) matching the stated cause on the beneficiary's own page. No report, off-cause spending or a vanished page stops the campaign and donors pull back the unreleased money pro-rata. Donors can attach evidence against fake reports.
